ABOUT

REIGNITE ESPORTS

Reignite Esports (RN) was founded in April 2026 with a clear ambition: to become one of the leading esports organizations by building a competitive ecosystem that connects talented players, passionate fans, and the next generation of esports enthusiasts.

Our focus is on developing competitive talent, fostering a strong community, creating professional opportunities, and expanding into emerging esports titles with discipline and competitive excellence at the core.
